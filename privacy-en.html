Publisher and scope

4Ever Keyboard is published under the name “4Ever Dj El Cubanito De Cienfuegos.” Privacy and support contact: suarezever502@gmail.com. This policy covers the keyboard. Applications you type or paste into, Windows, and Microsoft Store have their own practices and policies.

Information used and purposes

  • Typing and suggestions. The keyboard processes keys pressed on it and recent context to type into your chosen application and suggest words. It checks window and focus information to direct input and provide compatibility, such as with Premiere. Suggestions do not require importing your conversations.
  • Optional learning. When learning is enabled, the keyboard stores candidate words, language, counters, and word associations locally. Eligible words become learned suggestions after five exact uses; candidate records are stored before the fifth use to count repetitions. Learning can also use dictated text when enabled. You can add words manually.
  • Emails and phrases. Email addresses are added manually through setup or Saved emails. Saved phrases are retained for later insertion. This version does not automatically learn email addresses.
  • Microphone. Starting dictation records speech and transcribes it on your computer using local Whisper/whisper.cpp components. Temporary audio and transcript files are created. The app attempts to remove them on completion or closure; unexpected termination or permission errors may leave files behind. The latest dictation may remain in session memory for review.
  • Screenshots and clipboard. On request, the app captures the monitor containing the keyboard. Area selection temporarily uses the monitor image to choose a crop. The result is copied to the Windows clipboard. Paste sends a paste shortcut to your selected application. Save reads a clipboard image and, after confirmation, creates a JPEG in your chosen folder. Capturing does not automatically save an image file.
  • Preferences and errors. The app retains language, appearance, size, position, accessibility, sound, and learning settings. Application errors may create a local log containing a timestamp, exception type, and stack trace, potentially including technical paths. This is not an intentional record of everything you type.

Storage, protection, and retention

EXE version 4.4.8 stores user data under %LOCALAPPDATA%\4Ever Keyboard. Word memory, including emails, and saved phrases use Windows data protection associated with the current Windows user. Preferences and technical logs do not receive that same protection. Temporary audio/transcripts and JPEG files are not encrypted by the keyboard. Windows protection does not prevent access by every program running under your account.

Learned and manually added data persists between sessions until managed or removed. Local memory backups are retained. The EXE uninstaller preserves your user profile. JPEGs remain in their chosen folders. Clipboard retention and history are controlled by Windows. Synced folders and system backups may retain additional copies.

Transfers and other applications

The keyboard includes no account service, advertising, or automatic transmission of voice, vocabulary, or screenshots to its developer. Dictation and suggestions run locally. Typing and pasting deliver content to the application you select, which may store or transmit it. Clipboard history and synchronization, synced folders, and Microsoft Store services are subject to their providers and your settings.

If you send questions, diagnostics, or files to suarezever502@gmail.com, Ever Suarez will personally handle your request and receive your email address, message, and any files you share. They will be used to respond to and resolve your request. Ever will delete requests and their attachments from his support mailbox 90 days after resolution. Gmail, the email provider, processes messages under Google's practices. This period does not control copies held by the sender or the provider's technical retention. Do not send passwords or unnecessary confidential information.

Your controls and limitations

Suggestions and learning can be disabled separately in Settings. Disabling learning stops new observations but does not erase existing records. You can review learned or manually added words/emails and manage phrases and associations in their lists. Candidate records below the five-use threshold may not appear in the learned-word list.

In this version, deleting a word or email removes it from suggestions and retains a suppression marker containing its text. Backups may also contain earlier entries. This is not complete erasure from every file. A word can be learned again after further eligible uses.

To reset this EXE edition's entire profile, close the keyboard and remove its user-data folder identified above, including backups and temporary files. This also removes preferences and phrases. It does not remove JPEGs elsewhere, clipboard history, system backups, or content delivered to other applications, and is not forensic secure erasure. Contact privacy support for guidance; the developer has no remote access to your local memory through the keyboard.

Candidate filters reject numbers, symbols, and internal uppercase letters, but cannot recognize every password or confidential item. The keyboard is not a password manager. Disable learning before typing or dictating information you do not want retained.

Changes

We will update this policy when the described practices change and identify its effective date. Published policy and contact information must remain accessible.
